# Beamlet promotional video — storyboard

38.0 s / 1920×1080 / 60 fps / 2,280 frames. The video depicts only what is implemented: the Web / PWA version of Beamlet and `beamlet-stream` (the BOS1 optical stream). On-screen copy is in Japanese; the exact strings live in `source/src/scenes/`, and the table below gives English glosses.

## Art direction

- **Voice**: Tactical (precision instrumentation), Optical (high-luminance optical contrast), Cryptographic (cryptographic trust), Zero-Friction (immediacy). Avoid: generic SaaS purple gradients and meaningless sci-fi dummy-code decoration.
- **Typography**: Japanese headings and body in `"Hiragino Sans"` (W8/W6, natural phrase-level line breaks); Latin text, telemetry, and confirmation codes in `"SF Mono", Menlo` (wide tracking).
- **Color and contrast** (measured contrast ratios against the background `--bg: #05070d`): `--white: #ffffff` (**19.9:1 AAA**), `--text: #d7f7ff` (**17.8:1 AAA**), `--cyan: #3ef2ff` (**14.6:1 AAA**), `--ok: #5dff9a` (**15.4:1 AAA**), `--muted: #8aa4b4` (**7.8:1 AAA**), `--magenta: #ff3ec8` (**6.1:1 AA**).
- **Surface and restraint**: Following the design rules in `web/style.css`, the public-key QR plate the camera reads (`.plate`) and the sending frame (`#stage`) stay pure white `#ffffff` with `image-rendering: pixelated`; no scanline or grid decoration is layered on top.
